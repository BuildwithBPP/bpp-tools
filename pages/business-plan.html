The Operating Model

Three engines that feed each other

Revenue comes from three engines, not one. The strategic shift is rebalancing from pure time-for-money toward productized and recurring revenue — without abandoning the project work that wins relationships.

Engine 1
Time-Based
The three-tier ladder. Wins the relationship, generates the case study, funds everything else. The front door, not the destination.
Engine 2
Productized & Recurring
Care retainers (60–80% margin) + tool-only SaaS like the AI Proposal Agent ($99–299/mo, 80–90% margin). The compounding layer.
Engine 3
Free-Forever
Free Resource Library, plugins, YouTube (~3K subs), content. $0 direct revenue; every download is a lead. Compounds CAC down.

The flywheel. Internal builds become productized SKUs. SKUs throw off free fragments. Free resources generate leads. Leads become case studies. Case studies fund the next build. No single engine is the destination — the mix is.

The Market

The implementation gap is the opportunity

Only ~8–9% of small businesses have AI deeply deployed (Census BTOS), while 55–68% experiment ad-hoc with no system. That gap is what BPP closes. Market numbers below are corrected and sourced.

Florida employer firms517,775
SOM (3-yr, 0.5–1.5% of SAM)~250–800 clients

Two corrections from the old plan. "Over 50,000 Tampa businesses" is the ICP-qualified SAM, not the total (which is ~85–95K). And Florida's addressable base is 517,775 employer firms, not the 3.5M "small businesses" figure (~85% of which are solo and out of ICP).

Tailwinds: a steepening AI adoption S-curve in exactly this segment, rising labor costs pushing automation over headcount, documented ~150% first-year automation ROI, and the strategy gap itself as the demand.

Decision Gates

Risks with kill-criteria

Each risk names the threshold at which we change course rather than working harder at a broken model.

1 · H2 2026 ramp doesn't materialize
Trigger: if Q3 2026 closes below ~$10K, revert to the Low scenario, defer the first hire, and focus on highest-confidence pipeline.
2 · First case study doesn't land
Trigger: if no candidate closes at full price by end of Q3, take the smallest real win (Pilot or retainer) and document that instead.
3 · AI install demand fails externally
Trigger: if <2 paid Scale installs by end of Q1 2027, fold AI workflows into Operator milestones only.
4 · Care-retainer churn >20% in 6 months
Trigger: re-scope and re-price; fix the value framing behind "$500 resistance."
5 · Founder-delivery trap
Trigger: if an owner spends >50% of BPP hours on bespoke install work, pause install sales and tighten the playbook.
6 · Commoditization clock
Mitigation: assume the entry-tier floor keeps dropping; lean toward Operator, Scale, recurring, and local/vertical depth.
