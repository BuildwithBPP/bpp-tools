Tier 1Setup$599 launch
We teach, they do
One 90-minute working session. We get their AI account set up properly, make sure their files are somewhere sane (Google Drive, Teams, or OneDrive), and teach two or three real uses on their own work. They leave with a written cheat sheet.
Best for: the curious owner who wants to stop guessing and just get started.
Tier 2Workflows$1,999
We build it together
Three to four sessions. We build real working workflows side by side on their actual process, organize their file and folder structure properly, and leave them a cheat sheet plus 30 days of support. They can run and change it themselves afterward.
Best for: the owner who wants it working and wants to understand how it works.
Tier 3Pilot$2,997
We do it for them
Two weeks, hands-off for them. They pick one real problem that is costing money. We measure it, install the starter file and folder system, build one or two working automations live in their environment, then measure again and hand over a written before and after plus a recommendation on what to build next.
Best for: a larger or skeptical buyer who wants proof on their own business before committing to a full build.
Outcome They stop losing hours to busywork, and they have seen AI work on their own business rather than in a demo. Every tier is designed to end with them wanting the system built for real.
Credits up: Tier 2 fee credits toward any build package. Tier 3 credits 50% toward Scale or Operator. Tier 1 is the taste, it carries no credit.
Selling to a bigger company? Lead with Tier 3 and describe it as a two-week paid pilot rather than leaning on the Jumpstart name, which can sound entry level to a $2M owner.
