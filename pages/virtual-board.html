Decision Sparring

The BPP Virtual Board

An 8-voice sparring panel that pressure-tests the calls that matter. It routes 2 to 3 voices to your topic and hands back disagreement, not consensus. The board does not decide. It makes the owners defend their thinking against people who are not them.

"convene the board" "run this past the board" "what would the board say" /virtual-board

What it is

The Virtual Board is a sparring tool, not a decision-maker. The three owners still decide together. The board's job is to stress-test a decision before the team commits: route it to a small panel of distinct thinkers, each with a sharp and consistent lens, and get back the strongest version of the case against your plan. Where the panel disagrees is usually where the real risk sits.

The 8 voices

Every session routes 2 to 3 of these voices, picked to fit the topic. You never get all eight at once. You get the ones that will push hardest on this specific call.

Charlie Munger
Investor · Mental models
Invert the problem. What would guarantee this fails? Avoid stupidity before chasing brilliance.
Warren Buffett
Investor · Moats
Where is the durable advantage? Think in decades, and stay inside the circle of competence.
Andy Grove
Operator · Strategy
Only the paranoid survive. Is this an ordinary call, or a strategic inflection point?
Bill Campbell
Coach · People
What does this do to the team and the trust? The hard part is usually the people, not the plan.
Clayton Christensen
Theorist · Disruption
What job is the client hiring us to do? Watch for the disruption you are not pricing in.
Ben Horowitz
Operator · Hard calls
There is no recipe for the hard things. Make the tough call cleanly and own the consequences.
Naval Ravikant
Investor · Leverage
Where is the leverage? Play long-term games with long-term people, and reason from first principles.
Alex Hormozi
Operator · Offers & growth
Is the offer good enough that saying no feels stupid? Pressure on pricing, sales, and volume.

When to convene it

Offer the board before the team debates, any time a decision clears one of these bars:

Over the $2K lineAny spend or commitment above the $2K threshold.
Pricing or packagesChanging pricing, packages, or the ideal client profile.
Hiring or firingAdding a person to the team, or letting one go.
Off-ICP clientTaking on a client outside the ideal profile.
Operating-system shiftsChanging how BPP itself runs.

Three ways to run it

default

Sparring

Bring a live decision. The board routes the 2 to 3 voices that fit and spars until the disagreement is clear. The standard mode.

--quarterly

Quarterly review

Runs the six standing board questions. A scheduled health check on the business, not tied to a single decision.

--premortem

Premortem drill

The failure drill. Assume the decision already failed, then work backward to the story of how. Surfaces risks a forward look misses.

How a session flows

Bring a decision → Board routes 2-3 voices → They spar, you get disagreement → Owners decide together → Decision logged
It closes the loop. Decisions logged in a board session feed monday-decisions.json and surface automatically in the next Monday CEO brief. Nothing decided in a session gets lost, and nothing skips the owners. The board sharpens the thinking. The three owners still make the call.
