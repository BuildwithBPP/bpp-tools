The Content Cliff (June)
The content engine stopped in June, the same month revenue fell to $960.

Posts: 179 (Apr) → 144 (May) → 19 (Jun)
Views: 42,941 → 17,453 → 2,545

TikTok and LinkedIn published literally zero times in June. Whatever caused the stall, June is what turning the engine off looks like. The Marketing SOP + IdeaBank system (shipped Jul 7) is the structural fix; this chart is the argument for protecting it.

The TikTok formula, identified (post-level pull Jul 22): ONE video drove the breakout. Apr 25, the Emma Grede "Rule of Thirds" clip ("You're not supposed to be happy the whole time. That's the work.") did 28,631 views, 2,359 likes, 596 shares, with 95.8% of views from the For You feed. Runner-up was 320 views. Two OTHER Emma Grede clips flopped (25 and 36 views), so the formula is not the celebrity: it is the short, text-forward, emotionally universal founder-encouragement quote that people SHARE (596 shares fed the algorithm). Repeat that shape deliberately: one quotable line, founder emotion, zero pitch.

Q3 Outlook and Action Plan
What needs to happen next
Where We Are
Q2 cash revenue was $5,884 against a $15,000 target (39%), down 19% from Q1. That is two straight quarters missing the same target, and the same monthly shape both times: declining revenue, loss month at the end.

But the quarter was profitable ($1,180, 20% margin), expenses fell every month, and cash position improved ($7,687 in the bank on Jul 14 vs $5,220 in April, plus $1,793 A/R inbound). Savings crossed $3K and unlocked Phase 2 comp. The system is getting cheaper to run while revenue is the problem.

The Q2 OKRs mostly missed (2 of 13) because they were delivery-and-infrastructure goals while the constraint is revenue. The new sprint rule (only income or leads counts) is the correction.
Where We're Going
Fastest revenue available: collect the $2,340 already earned. Both blockers (Rodney's HALO plan, the Lois AI scope) are internal. Nothing to sell, just finish.

Pipeline is $11,497 with three proposals out. Flava Meets Fusion ($4,497) and HALO Retainer ($3,000) are the two that change the quarter. The retainer is the first test of recurring revenue.

TikTok found a formula in late April. Repeat it deliberately. The website grew all quarter without social; find the Jun 11-13 driver and pour into what works.

Set 2 OKRs for Q3, not 4. Both revenue-gated. Score them monthly, not at quarter end.

1. Collect the $2,340. Rodney finishes the HALO plan; Daunte + Kenny scope the Lois AI build. Both invoices out within 2 weeks.

2. Decide the FA quarter. Financial Acuity's $3,350 is currently counted in both Q1 and Q2. Pick one so annual reporting doesn't double-count.

3. Fix the follow-up leak. 4 of 12 lost deals stopped communicating. Repair HubSpot sequence tracking (pay for help or call HubSpot) and make follow-up a weekly standing task.

4. Close the carried Q1 items or kill them. QB recategorization, Engagement Type backfill, Clockify, resource page. Each gets a commit-or-kill decision at the meeting. No third carry.

5. Small leaks: cancel Gamma ($10/mo since the April cut decision), delete the HubSpot "test" deal, match the $444 Unapplied Cash, get Rodney's W-9 before the next payment (1099-NEC will trigger).
