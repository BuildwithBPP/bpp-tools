How to Use This

Sprint Pulse Cadence

  1. Every Friday, Eli and Kenny fill in self-report fields before the weekly stand-up call.
  2. Claude pulls the latest HubSpot data (contacts, sequence stats, deals) and updates the pulse document.
  3. The team reviews the pulse together on the call — scoreboard, pipeline, friction points, template feedback.
  4. After the call, the finalized HTML is saved here and in the BPP workspace at 1. Internal Operations/2. Marketing & Sales/Sprint Pulse/.
  5. At the start of any new sprint or when Claude needs historical context, it reads _claude/context/sprint-pulse-log.md for a fast summary of all past pulses.
