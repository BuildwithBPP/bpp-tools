The Problem

Our service ladder ends abruptly

When a client wraps Launch Pad, Operator System, or Scale System and isn't ready for the next package, we don't have a formal product to offer. Today the gap is filled informally — a 30-day "support window" baked into closeouts (live right now for SEEDFOLKids through 2026-06-20) plus a single $750/mo "Retainer option" mentioned in passing in the SEEDFOLKids closeout email. That $750 figure was arbitrary and likely too high for our ICP. Most BPP clients can't justify $1K/mo; $250 is closer to what's defensible.

Why now · 1
Keep the client relationship warm between project phases instead of letting it go cold.
Why now · 2
Generate predictable MRR. A $4,500 closeout becoming an ongoing $300-$500/mo smooths our lumpy project income.
Why now · 3
Soften the next-phase pitch. A $4,500 or $10K quote is a big jump; a Care Plan is a low-friction "stay close" alternative.
Sprint North Star
Reduce friction to close, close more deals. Care Plan directly serves the sprint 2-4 sales goal.

Pitfalls that kill low-ticket retainers

  • 1
    Undefined scope → unlimited expectation → margin death
  • 2
    No response-time SLA → "I emailed you Tuesday" friction
  • 3
    No monthly cadence ritual → feels invisible → churn at month 3
  • 4
    Underdelivering at low tier → erodes brand trust → kills upsell path
  • 5
    Free creep from project clients ("you'd just be answering my questions") → cannibalizes the paid tier
The Proposal

Three tiers, one product

Called the "BPP Care Plan" — not "Retainer." "Retainer" reads as fancy/expensive to our ICP. "Care Plan" reads as "thing I'm comfortable paying $30 for at my WordPress host." Lower buying friction at the price point we're targeting.

Tier 1
Care Plan Lite
$249/mo
"Stay in the family with light hands-on support"
Included
  • Monthly website backup + hosting check
  • Quarterly brand guidelines refresh (1 update)
  • 1 hour/month of execution time — any capability area (see below)
  • Email + text — async, 48-hr SLA
  • Month-to-month, 30-day cancel
10% credit toward any future BPP package (caps at $500)
Tier 2
Care Plan Standard
$499/mo
"Where most clients land when wrapping Operator or Scale"
Everything in Lite, plus
  • Monthly 30-min strategy call (Daunte / Kenny / Eli rotate)
  • 3 hours/month of execution time (vs. 1 in Lite)
  • Monthly 1-page snapshot of their KPIs
  • 24-hr SLA on email + text
  • Multi-step work across CRM, AI, QB, web, brand, data
15% credit toward future BPP package (caps at $1,000)
Tier 3
Care Plan Plus
$899/mo
"BPP as a fractional ops/marketing team between phases"
Everything in Standard, plus
  • 5 hours/month of execution (vs. 3)
  • Bi-weekly strategy calls (vs. monthly)
  • Quarterly executive review (Daunte attends, deck-format)
  • One named AI workflow build per quarter
  • Light financial planning support included
20% credit toward future BPP package (caps at $2,000)
Capability Areas

What execution hours can be spent on

The Care Plan's execution time is channel-agnostic across BPP's full skillset. Clients can spend hours on CRM optimization, AI workflows, QuickBooks maintenance, web edits, brand assets, data work, light financial support, or training. The right BPP lead is assigned based on the work type.

Why this matters: the Care Plan isn't a website-only product. It's a maintenance + incremental-improvement layer on top of everything BPP delivers in Operator System and Scale System engagements.

Scope Discipline

What the Care Plan is NOT

State explicitly in the sales sheet and the contract. This is exactly where low-ticket retainers die.

Not included — say so up front
  • Not a re-do of the original engagement
  • Not unlimited support — capped hours are capped
  • Not a substitute for Operator System or Scale System when scope grows
  • Anything beyond the cap triggers a written change order at BPP's standard project rate
  • Strategy is scoped to maintenance / triage, not new initiatives

Verification (DMADV "V")

How we'll know it worked

Treat this as a 90-day pilot. We measure, we learn, we iterate or kill. Verify criteria below are the contract we hold ourselves to.

30-day check · first signed client
  • Sign at Standard tier without price push-back?
  • Actual time spent inside the 3-hour cap?
  • Monthly snapshot ship on time?
90-day pilot review
  • Total MRR added across all signed Care Plans
  • Hours-delivered vs. hours-promised (margin check)
  • Churn count
  • Conversions back into a full package — the leading indicator
Kill criteria
  • Avg hours delivered > 1.5× cap → scope failed, restructure or kill
  • 2+ clients churn in 60 days → pricing/positioning wrong
  • Zero full-package conversions in 6 months → it's a holding pen, not a flywheel
Out of Scope (For v1)

What we deliberately ruled out

  • Outcome-based pricing — we don't have the attribution data to support this cleanly. Revisit in 2027 if Care Plan v2 wants a performance tier.
  • Industry-specific Care Plans — premature segmentation. Start with the three universal tiers.
  • Replacing the upgrade credits structure — they stack, not replace.
  • Building the actual sales sheet, contract, and delivery playbook — that's the next ask after team consensus.
