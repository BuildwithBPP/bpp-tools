Run pre-call prep

Use the discovery-call-prep workflow to research the lead and enter the conversation with a decision-ready brief.

Trigger
“Run pre-call prep for [prospect].”
Inputs
Prospect name, company, industry, referral source, presenter name, CRM record, and ICP criteria.
Output
Fit, risks, hypotheses, questions, and a recommended package.
Destination
Use in the call and attach notes to the HubSpot record.
Workflow action available in Claude

Create a discovery deck

Turn the call brief into a client-facing conversation deck when the opportunity needs one.

Trigger
“Create a discovery deck for [company].”
Inputs
Decision-ready call brief, offer context, and meeting goal.
Output
Editable discovery deck.
Destination
Review before sharing with the prospect.
Workflow action available in Claude

Draft a proposal

Prepare a scoped proposal only after discovery confirms the offer and next step.

Trigger
“Draft a proposal for [company] using these call notes.”
Inputs
Call notes, approved package, scope, pricing, and timeline.
Output
Proposal draft for owner review.
Destination
Review, then log the next step in HubSpot.
Workflow action available in Claude
