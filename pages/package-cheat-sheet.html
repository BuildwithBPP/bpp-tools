BPP Sales Cheat Sheet

Everyone pitches the same way. Money first, plain English, no jargon. If you can only remember one thing: lead with the money or the time, name their problem in their words, then name the package.

Start here — what "the AI operating system" actually is (for a non-technical owner)

Say it like this: "Think about everything that keeps your business running — following up with leads, tracking customers, sending invoices, knowing your numbers, all the repetitive admin. Right now most of that lives in your head, or scattered across sticky notes, texts, and spreadsheets. We build one organized place where all of it lives and runs — your files, your process, your numbers — and we put AI to work inside it so the busywork happens on its own. It's the difference between a business that depends on you remembering everything, and one that runs itself while you sell and grow."

What it is under the hood (internal only — never say this to a client): the file/folder system we run BPP on (our Cowork setup) + Claude skills + automations. Clients get a sized-up version at every tier. To the client, always describe it by what it does, never by the plumbing.

The money translation (always close the loop to this): fewer dropped leads = more deals closed · faster admin = more hours to sell · clear numbers = you stop leaking money you can't see. Organized businesses make more and keep more. That's the whole pitch.
Never open with "AI operating system," "operating system," "file structure," "automations," or any tool name. Those are how we build it, not why they buy it. Lead with the dollar or the hour. Every time. Bring in "operating system" only once they're nodding and ask "so what do you actually do?"

The 3-line pitch — works for any package

1. The money/time hook: "Most businesses like yours are leaking money they can't see — slow follow-ups, no system, no clear picture of what's profitable."
2. Their problem in their words: "Sounds like everything falls apart when you step away / leads slip through the cracks / you're buried in admin instead of selling."
3. The package as the fix: "That's exactly what our [package] fixes — here's how it works and what you'd get." Then use the package block below.

How they climb — every rung feeds the next

AI Jumpstart (learn it) → Growth & AI Pilot / Launch Pad (prove it / start it) → Operator (run on it) → Scale (grow on it). Coaching teaches them; the packages build it for them. The credits are the escalator: Next Level → Pilot/Launch Pad, Launch Pad → 50% into Operator, Operator → $1,000 into Scale, Pilot → 50% into Scale. Never oversell the top.
