BPP Package Cheat Sheet

Memorize this. Everyone pitches the same way. Money first, no jargon.

What BPP is — say it exactly like this

"We help you make more money and take back your time by fixing the behind-the-scenes stuff — follow-up, customer tracking, your numbers — and letting smart tools do the busywork."
If they ask how: "Most growing businesses leak money they can't see: slow follow-ups, no system, no clear picture of what's profitable. We plug those leaks and put automation and AI to work, so more of what you're already doing turns into revenue, and less of it depends on you."
Never say "AI operating system," "operating system," "file structure," or any tool name up front. Lead with the money or the time. Every time.

Entry on-ramp — AI Jumpstart (coaching)

Say this: "Learn to run your busywork on AI in a few sessions — proposals, emails, scheduling, pricing." · Setup $599 (90-min 1:1) or Next Level $1,999 (3–4 sessions + a cheat sheet they keep + 30 days support). Best for existing clients and solo owners who want to do it themselves with a coach.
This is the front door, and it leads straight into the operating system: coaching gets them using AI on their own; when they want it built for real, we install it (Growth & AI Pilot → Operator → Scale), and the Next Level fee credits toward a Pilot or Launch Pad. Pilot it with HALO, Financial Acuity, and Wanda first.

What the "AI operating system" actually is (internal only)

It's the file/folder system we run BPP on (our Cowork setup) plus Claude skills and automations. Clients get a version of it at every tier, sized up as they climb. The Growth & AI Pilot is the starter install of it: the file/folder system plus a few working skills/automations on one real problem, so they see it running in their own business before the full build. Operator layers on the CRM and more automations; Scale is the full command center, dashboards, and 5–8 automations. To the client, describe it by what it does (fewer dropped balls, faster work, clear numbers), never by the plumbing.

Scale System — full scope · $10,000–$15,000 · 8–12 weeks · fixed price (custom above $15K)

Foundation (always first)

  • Full process audit — 3–5 key processes mapped, gaps found, optimization roadmap
  • SOP documentation — any team member can execute without the owner

Core deliverables

  • BI dashboard — Power BI / Looker, auto-refresh KPIs, margin by service, cash flow
  • 2–4 advanced AI automations — multi-step, cross-tool
  • Financial planning — 12-month model, scenario planning
  • Advanced CRM optimization — on what they already have

If needed (not assumed)

  • Custom AI tool — proposal builder / client front-end / gov-contractor workflow
  • Website rebuild or Webflow migration — only if the site blocks growth
  • Full CRM migration — only if the current CRM is unsalvageable

Delivery & support

  • 3–4 training sessions (2 hrs), team + leadership
  • 60-day priority email + Teams support
  • 3-month check-in cadence; full playbook handoff

How to run any pitch

Money first. First sentence is a dollar or time outcome. Always.
Name their problem in their words before you name a package.
Size to the business, not the biggest number — credits pull them up.
Hesitant on Scale? Lead with the Pilot. Proof beats persuasion.
