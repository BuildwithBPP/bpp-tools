Client Story · Operator & Scale Systems

From midnight proposals to a $32M bid, fully verified

Xtremely Clean Janitorial Services · Woman-owned commercial cleaning, Tampa FL · $2M+ revenue · Client since 2025

The before

Founder Wanda Barton has 25+ years in the industry and a business doing over $2M a year. But she wrote every proposal personally, from scratch, often late into the night: 2 to 4 hours each, no rate card, no templates, pricing based on competitors' numbers. Bids, renewals, and insurance deadlines lived in her head. Her sales rep had leads but no system: no assignments, no follow-up tracking, no accountability.

What we built

  • An AI proposal engine trained on her own past bids: her best language, pricing rules, and compliance sections in a reusable library
  • HubSpot CRM with dual pipelines (commercial + government), a 10-stage municipal pipeline, renewal + compliance reminders, and templates simple enough her rep actually uses them
  • A lead-assignment + follow-up system for her sales team ("here are your 100 leads, assigned, now follow up") — now BPP's internal model for other clients
  • Forensic lead audit: caught an entirely fabricated lead list from her own rep (555 phones, sequential addresses); salvaged 34 real hotels + 15 companies
  • Bid templates and a pricing kit on her real cost math
  • Beyond scope: fixed her email rules problem, ran a data-exposure security check, and wrote a full business plan for her second company (De-Dior Salon Studios — also Kenny's first closed deal)

The proof moment

March 2026: a bid for a 7-year airport janitorial contract worth over $32M with a 75-person staffing plan. BPP built the proposal end to end, then audited the final 89-page draft hours before the 3:30 PM deadline: 66-point automated cross-check, 66 passed, 0 failed, catching a blank Price Proposal form (the primary scoring document) plus blank required affidavits — automatic-disqualification issues, caught in time. Status: submitted, award pending. Never say "won". Also: a 2024 St. Pete airport ranking in her files is HER pre-BPP bid — never cite it.

A ~$3.3K project in 2025 became an ongoing advisory relationship 14+ months later, now spanning two of her companies.

Client Story · Nonprofit Brand + Systems

A nonprofit that became findable, fundable, and financially clean

SEEDFOLKids (What We Could Be Exchange, 501c3) · Youth garden & entrepreneurship program, Tampa FL · 13-week engagement, 2026

The before

The kids' honey had a loyal Saturday following at Ybor Market, but online the program was invisible: no independent website, not ranking on Google, no settled brand, volunteers on a ~100-person paper list. Duplicate bookkeeping entries made 2025 income look like $91K when the real number was about $56K, which matters when nonprofit filing requirements change at revenue thresholds.

What we built in 13 weeks

  • Full brand: strategy, guidelines, complete logo suite
  • Independent website with a working online honey store and tested checkout
  • Donation system with a $50K goal bar
  • CRM + volunteer intake replacing the paper list
  • SEO + Google presence: now ranks for its own name
  • Three fiscal years of QuickBooks cleanup

The results

The cleanup revealed what inflated books had been hiding: a real ~$40K-a-year commerce operation run by elementary school kids (~$30K card + ~$10K cash honey sales, corrected 2025). Findable, donatable, and shoppable online for the first time. Numbers now trustworthy for any donor, CPA, or grant committee.

Client Story · Launch Pad (Foundation Tier)

Thirty years of credibility, finally visible online

Financial Acuity Coaching · Financial coaching for women approaching retirement · Foundation build, closed 2026

The before

Maxine Cholmondeley's credentials are serious: 20+ years as a real estate attorney, 12 years in bankruptcy counseling. But Financial Acuity had no digital presence to convert interest into booked clients. No website, no Google presence, no intake system. Her clients said "eye-opening" and "changed how I view my financial future." Nobody searching could find any of it.

What we built

  • 3-page website with intake questionnaire, mobile-optimized, on her own domain
  • Booking system for free intro calls with automated questionnaire follow-up
  • Google Business Profile optimized for local search
  • Complete brand kit: logo suite, guidelines, colors, business cards
  • Her clients' real testimonials, collected and published

The result

A coach with 30 years of credibility now looks like it online. A stranger can discover Financial Acuity, read real client stories, and book a call without Maxine touching a thing.
