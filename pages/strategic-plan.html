The Flywheel

How the engines feed each other

No single engine is the destination — the mix is. Internal builds become productized SKUs. SKUs throw off free-resource fragments. Free resources generate leads. Leads become case studies. Case studies fund the next internal build.

The compounding loop

1. Build for ourselves. Internal AI Workforce — the 6 AI Employees, the plugins, the Cowork stack.

2. Productize. The same workflows become Install + Care SKUs ($3-8K install + a monthly retainer, pricing in research) and tool-only subscriptions ($99-499/mo).

3. Free fragments. Pieces of the productized stack become free templates and downloadable kits — Cowork Starter Kit, KPI Dashboard Template, Monthly Close Checklist.

4. Leads. Free resources + ManyChat + plugin marketplace + content compound into qualified inbound.

5. Case studies. Several in parallel — Financial Acuity, Lois Marketing, and a flagship install client. Proof becomes the moat. No single case carries it.

6. Fund the next build. Cash from cases pays for the next internal AI Workforce iteration. Loop tightens.

Owner Compensation · Ratified 2026-05-17

What the team actually decided — kept simple on purpose

The earlier draft proposed a phased framework with profit-share percentages and escalating draws. At the 2026-05-17 Weekly Business Meeting all three owners reviewed it and simplified it. Two things start now; real profit sharing waits for runway. The point: reward the work without starving the business.

Effective Now · Component 1
5% Sales Commission
Per closed deal · to the lead source / closer
5% of the closed deal value goes to whoever sourced the lead and closed it. A cap applies (default $750/deal) so one large deal doesn't distort the pool.

Examples: $4,500 Operator deal × 5% = $225 to closer. A $12K install × 5% = $600. A $15K+ deal hits the $750 cap. This is the real incentive — sell more, earn more.
Effective Now · Component 2
$150/month Guaranteed Payment
Per partner · mid-month cadence
$150/month per partner, starting immediately (first payments already sent). Deliberately modest — keeps the business saving toward runway while still rewarding the work.

Kenny and Eli take theirs in cash. Daunte defers his — booked as a balance-sheet liability and repaid once cash supports it. Each owner sets aside 30% personally for taxes (these payments are taxable income, ~15.3% SE tax + regular rate).
Later · Savings-Gated
Real Profit Sharing
Trigger: savings clears ~3 months of runway
Current savings (~$3K) is about 1.5 months of expenses. Before larger payouts, the team builds the emergency fund to ~3 months. Then the $150/mo guaranteed payment scales up and true profit sharing begins.

Eventual aspiration discussed: ~$1,000/month per partner. Percentages and cadence to be set when the trigger is hit — not pre-committed now.

Why the team kept it simple: The phased framework with fixed profit-share percentages assumed numbers the business hasn't proven yet. Two live components (commission + a small guaranteed payment) reward the work immediately. The bigger payouts stay tied to a real milestone — 3 months of runway — instead of a forecast. Reward the work without starving the business. Daunte deferring his own payment keeps cash in the business while Kenny and Eli, who need it more right now, get paid.
