Clean launch

  • 40 qualified cleaning owners

  • One message and one paid audit

  • Maximum 12 owner-hours and $250 cash spend

Find the working path

  • Complete 80 qualified cleaning-account exposures
  • Run audit readouts within the 30-day credit window
  • Track reply-to-call and call-to-audit conversion

  • Record acquisition and delivery hours

Earn expansion

  • 120 qualified cleaning accounts completed
  • 40 referral partners worked
  • 8 qualified conversations
  • 3 paid audits
  • 1 full-price $5,500 Operator
  • One quantified result or case study

Short, problem-led, and specific. Use the prospect's world. Do not lead with BPP, AI, CRM, or an all-in-one promise.

Subject: [first], bid follow-up

[First], saw [specific contract, hiring, territory, or vendor-registration trigger].

Quick question: when [Company] pursues larger commercial contracts, where does the process leak most often: walkthrough scheduling, proposal turnaround, or follow-up after the quote?

We help Tampa cleaning operators map that chain and find the contracts or margins falling through it. Worth sending over the five-point audit outline?

Email 2 / cleaning proof

Subject: proposal follow-up

One reason I asked: we helped a Tampa janitorial operator replace owner-memory with separate commercial and government pipelines, proposal follow-up, and bid checks.

The point was not more software. It was fewer contracts dying between walkthrough and decision.

Does [Company] already have that chain visible in one place?

Internal one-to-one proof only. The $32M airport bid package was submitted and verified. Never say won.

Email 3 / resource offer

Subject: cleaning owner checklist

We built a 25-point Run-Without-You checklist specifically for commercial cleaning owners. It covers bids, renewals, crew handoffs, account margins, and the systems that usually live in the owner's head.

I can send the PDF if useful. Which section matters more right now: sales follow-up or margin by account?

I'll close the loop after this.

If bid follow-up or account-margin visibility becomes a priority at [Company], reply “audit” and I'll send the five-point scope.

If I have the wrong person, a name would help. Either way, I won't keep filling your inbox.

Objection responses
“Send information.”

Absolutely. Is the bigger concern missed follow-up, proposal turnaround, or margin by account? I'll send the section that matches.

“We already have a CRM.”

Good. This is not a CRM sale. We test whether the walkthrough, proposal, follow-up, renewals, and account margins actually connect.

“$1,000 is too much.”

If one missed contract or underpriced account is not worth $1,000, we should not sell you the audit. Is the problem too small, or is the value still unclear?

“I need to think.”

Of course. What specifically needs thinking: priority, trust, or cash?

“We need more leads.”

Maybe. But if proposals and follow-up leak, more leads amplify the waste. We should measure the handoff first.

“Not right now.”

Understood. What event would make this a priority: a new contract, another crew, or a margin problem? I'll set one real follow-up date.
