Retainer Enhancement Ideas
Structural Upgrades
Response Time SLAs by Tier
Ladder the SLAs so higher tiers feel premium. Example: Launch Maintenance → 48hr, Operator Maintenance → 24hr, AI Ops → 4hr urgent / same-day standard. From the BPP contract template; adds zero cost, high perceived value.
Minimum Commitment Period
Launch Maintenance stays cancel-anytime (low friction to start). Operator + AI Ops retainers get a 3-month minimum. Reduces churn, helps BPP plan capacity. Industry standard at this price range.
Rollover vs. Fixed Model
Currently unused services expire monthly. Option: unused services roll forward 1 month, then expire. Reduces "I didn't use it" cancellations without creating unlimited liability for BPP. Worth deciding before retainers scale.
Dedicated Communication Channel
Ladder by tier: Launch Maintenance = email only; Operator Maintenance = dedicated Slack/Teams channel; AI Ops = Slack channel + monthly 30-min video call. High perceived value (VIP feel), minimal actual lift.
Value-Add Ideas
Priority Queue — Make It Explicit
"Retainer clients skip the project queue for any new scope request." Currently buried in Operator Maintenance — should be front-and-center in all three tiers. This is the thing that makes retainers feel like a VIP arrangement, not a support ticket.
Referral Credit for Retainer Clients
$250 credit per closed referral — applied toward next month's retainer OR toward upgrading their package. From the closeout tier data. Keeps retainer clients as active referral sources without feeling transactional.
Annual Strategic Session (AI Ops tier)
For $1,500-$2,000/mo clients: include a 90-min annual session reviewing what was built, what's changed in the business, and what the next-year roadmap looks like. Also the natural upgrade conversation. Positions BPP as a strategic partner, not just a vendor.
Monthly Business Scorecard
Instead of a generic "monthly performance report," deliver a branded 1-page PDF each month showing the 3-5 KPIs that matter to that specific client. Tangible artifact that justifies the retainer every single month — makes the value visible even in quiet months.

12 Free Assets by EOY 2026 — Engine 3
Each asset does one job: generate a lead for a specific tier. These are the top of the funnel for the entire package system.

The strategic plan's Engine 3 goal is 12 free-forever assets by EOY 2026. Every asset maps to a tier and routes the right buyer to the right package. The two highest-leverage assets — the ROI Calculator and the "Which package is right for you?" quiz — should ship first. They qualify buyers AND route them automatically.

Lead Capture Rules — How Each Asset Handles Email
Gated (email required): All PDFs, Notion templates, and downloadable resources. When someone submits their email → asset delivers automatically via email sequence (HubSpot or ConvertKit). Their email goes into BPP CRM tagged by which asset they downloaded, so follow-up is automatic and relevant.

Not gated: Automation ROI Calculator — let them use it freely; the value is in the result, not the gate. Add a soft CTA at the end ("Want us to build these automations for you?").

Quiz → Discovery Call CTA: "Which BPP Package Is Right For You?" is not gated mid-quiz, but the result screen gates the full recommendation behind a name + email field. After submitting: (1) quiz result emails to the visitor, (2) their responses + recommended package auto-log in HubSpot, (3) the result page shows a "Book a Free Discovery Call" button linked to Calendly. This replaces the cold "book a call" ask with a warm, self-qualified lead who already knows which package fits them.
