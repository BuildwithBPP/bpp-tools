1My goal

The best realistic outcome. Just one. Package, price, terms, start date, and the next step with a date. Think about the worst case, but don't write it down. Say this out loud to a teammate before the call.

We walk away if: no revenue or team yet · not a service business · they won't use what we build · they expect a big discount to start.

2What I know say it back near the end

Their situation in their words, two or three sentences. You want "that's right." ("You're right" means they've stopped listening.) Then ask "Did I miss something?"

3Accusation audit aim for 6

Every bad thing they might think about us or this call. Say the big ones early, before they can. "You're probably thinking ___."

4Labels aim for 5

What you think they're feeling. Say it, then stop talking. "It seems like ___." "It sounds like ___." "It looks like ___."

Based on Chris Voss, Never Split the Difference, and the Black Swan Group prep sheetv1 · 2026-09-27 · page 1 of 2

5Questions 4 What · 3 How · 2 no-oriented · 1 why

Cover all three: what they want ("What are we trying to fix here?"), who else decides ("Who else has a say in something like this?"), what doing nothing costs ("What happens if you do nothing?").

No-oriented "Would it be crazy to ___?" "Are you against ___?"
Why (proof of life) "Why us, and not ___?" If they can't answer, you may just be the comparison quote.

6What could be hiding

The thing you don't know yet that could kill the deal.

  • Someone else decides (partner, spouse, lender)
  • Cash timing (slow season, a client pays late)
  • Another quote, or hiring instead
  • They only want advice, not the build
  • Burned by a consultant or software before

What doesn't add up? · What do they lose if nothing changes?

7Their likely worries

Follow-up labels. "It seems like you're worried that ___."

9Saying no, keeping them

  1. "How am I supposed to do that?"
  2. "I'm afraid that doesn't work for me."
  3. "I'm sorry, no."

Not a fit: "It seems like we're not the right fit right now. Where do you want to go from here?"

10What they can give besides money

  • A Google review when we finish
  • Their story as a case study
  • An intro to one or two owners they know
  • A reference call for a future client
  • A decision by a set date

11The close

Before you hang up, get them to agree three times:

"What are the next steps?"
"How will we know we're on track?"
"What should we do if we get off track?"
