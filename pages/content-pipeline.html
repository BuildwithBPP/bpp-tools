Idea Stage 1

Content Lead reviews Idea Vault (oldest-first). Promising ideas get flipped to Approved. Everything else gets Parked or deleted. Nothing leaves this review still at Idea status.

BPP Media Drive — Folder Convention
Per-Video Folder Naming
Format
[##]_(FirstName) - Exact Notion Title — always two-digit order number, first name in parens, title copy-pasted exact (no trailing punctuation changes)
Example
03_(Eli) - Project Management Isn’t a Tool You Buy
Subfolders
01_Raw — iPhone footage  ·  02_Finals — exported cuts  ·  03_Thumbs — cover photo options
Drive Link
Copy the link to the per-video subfolder (not the parent content day folder) and paste it into the Notion Drive Link property. Editor clicks directly to their footage.
Final Export File Naming
Format
BPP_[YYYY-MM-DD]_[short-title]_[platform].mp4
Example
BPP_2026-07-15_relationships-close-deals_IG.mp4
BPP Media/ 02_Content Days/ 2026/ 2026-07-15/ 01_(Kenny) - Relationships Close Deals and Systems Keep Them/ 01_Raw/ 02_Finals/ BPP_2026-07-15_relationships-close-deals_IG.mp4 03_Thumbs/
Series Rules
Series = A Named Campaign with a Concrete Plan
  • Only activate a series when you have 3+ ideas planned for it. Create all 3+ Notion entries at once, tag them with the same Series name, and put them On Deck together.
  • Film as a batch. All ideas in a series get shot on the same content day and released in consecutive weeks.
  • Name it specifically. “Tools That Saved Us 10 Hours This Week” is a real campaign. “Tools That Are 2 Good” as an infinite bucket is not.
  • Leave Series blank for standalone pieces. Don’t tag an idea as a series “in case it becomes one.”
  • Existing series (1 min Biz Tip, Tools That Are 2 Good, Founder Psychology) are dormant — the options stay in the dropdown, but nothing should be tagged to them unless you’re actively running that campaign with a 3-part plan in hand.
Stale Ideas Framework
Monthly Rule — Every Idea Gets a Decision
When
Every month, the week before content day, when the Content Lead opens Idea Vault to build On Deck.
Rule
Work through Idea Vault oldest-first. Every entry at Status = Idea gets one decision: Promote, Park, or Delete. Nothing leaves the session still at Idea.
Promote → Approved
Team wants to make it. Serves a Pillar, has a clear hook. Flip to Approved and it becomes eligible for On Deck next cycle.
Park → Parked
Good concept, wrong season. Disappears from active views. Revisited at the quarterly purge.
Delete
Not good, already covered, or will never use. Gone. The bar for deletion is “we’d never add this today.”
Quarterly Hard Purge (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct)
  • Review everything in Parked. Test: “If this wasn’t already in the database, would you add it today?”
  • No → delete it. Yes → move back to Idea for the next monthly cycle.
  • Anything Parked for 6+ months (two consecutive quarters) gets deleted automatically.
