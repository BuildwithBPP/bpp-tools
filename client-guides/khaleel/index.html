The Big Picture

Before any setup, two ideas worth a minute. Get these and the rest is just clicks.

You install one thing: Claude Desktop. Inside it you'll use two modes that share the same workspace folder.

Heavy Lifter

Think of it like Your truck has a daily-driver gear and a tow mode. Same truck, different jobs. You don't install a second truck. Same with Claude Desktop: Chat mode and Cowork mode are two views of the one app.

Idea 2: Three pieces inside the workspace folder

Three things sit inside the folder. They're the difference between a smart chatbot and a real operator.

CLAUDE.md (the brain). One file at the top of your workspace. Tells Claude who you are, how you talk, what Cape to the Bay does, and what you don't want.

Think of it like The scope of work you'd hand a new crew member on day one. Who runs this job, what the rules are, what's in bounds, what's out. They read it once and they know.

The _claude/context/ folder (the SOP binder). Companion files: your business model, your brand voice, your writing style, your operating norms. Claude reads them every session too.

Think of it like Your standard-operating-procedures binder on the office shelf. The crew references it when they need to know how you spec a tile install or how you handle a change order. Claude does the same.

The _claude/skills/ folder (your templates). Five saved workflows. Each one runs the same job the same way every time, off a trigger phrase.

Think of it like Your favorite scope template. You don't draft a new walkthrough doc from scratch every job. You start from the template, swap in the details. A skill is that template, except Claude executes it for you.

The Setup, In Order

Five steps. About 30 minutes total. Do them in this order and nothing else needs explaining.

1 Get Google Drive On Your Laptop10 minutes. The filing cabinet.

Your business files already live in Google Drive: photos, scopes, sheets. We need them to also show up on your laptop like a regular folder so Claude can read them. Google has a free app that does this.

Think of it like Drive is your office filing cabinet. The app puts a remote to that cabinet on your laptop. The files stay in Drive. You just get to open them like they're on your desk.
  1. Go to google.com/drive/download
  2. Click "Download Drive for desktop." Run the installer.
  3. Sign in with the same Google account that has your business Drive (probably khaleel@capetobay.com).
  4. When it asks how to store files, pick "Stream files". Files pull down only when you open them. Saves laptop space.

Did it work?

Open Windows File Explorer. You should see a new drive letter (usually G: or H:) labeled "Google Drive." Click into it and you'll see "My Drive" with everything from your Drive inside.

If you don't see it, check the bottom-right corner of Windows for the Drive icon. Click it, and it'll tell you what's wrong.

2 Drop The Workspace Folder Into Drive5 minutes. Where Claude reads from.

I sent you a zip file called capetobay-workspace-template.zip. Unzip it and drop the folder inside your Google Drive (anywhere under G:\My Drive\ works). I'll call its new home G:\My Drive\CapeToBay-Workspace from here on.

Think of it like I built you a new drawer for the filing cabinet, pre-labeled and pre-filled. You're just sliding it into the cabinet.

The Five Workflows In Your WorkspacePre-built. Just say the trigger phrase.

Each skill has a trigger phrase, a script Claude follows, and a known output location. Full details at _claude/skills/[skill-name]/SKILL.md. Index at _claude/skills/SKILL-DICTIONARY.md.

  1. 1. Business card scanner

    The new one. Snap a card photo at BNI / Star Assoc / Simple Happenings. Claude extracts the contact, saves it to your pipeline, and preps it for Housecall Pro import. No more cards in your truck console.

    Trigger: "scan this card" or "new business card from [event]"
    Paste or upload the card image. Claude extracts name, company, phone, email, where you met, and your notes. Appends a row to contacts.csv and logs the contact to memory.
    Result: Card saved, ready for Housecall Pro bulk import. Next-action suggestion included.
  2. 2. Lead followup

    Drafts personalized followups in batches for warm leads and stale referrals. Per your operating norms: referrals get a real reply, fast. Silence kills future referrals.

    Trigger: "lead followup" or "let's work the warm leads"
    Paste a list of leads or describe who to work. Claude drafts in your voice, references the referrer by name, gives one clear ask per message.
    Result: 10-20 personalized drafts in 5 minutes. You review and send.
  3. 3. Walkthrough to proposal

    Same workflow you do with ChatGPT today, just sharper and the file lands in the right client folder automatically. Voice notes plus photos become a scope-of-work Word doc.

    Trigger: "walkthrough to proposal" or "scope this job"
    Provide voice notes (transcript or audio) + photos. Claude builds the project summary, scope by area, pricing draft, timeline, and assumptions. Outputs to 2. Clients/{name}/Project/.
    Result: Draft scope within 24 hours of the walkthrough. Polish window: 48 hours.
  4. 4. Meeting notes

    After any BNI weekly, prospect call, partner call, or walkthrough debrief. Decisions, action items with owners and dates, referrals exchanged, money discussed.

    Trigger: "meeting notes" or "write up that call"
    Paste a transcript or just tell Claude what happened. File goes to the right folder automatically (BNI → networking, client → client comms, etc.).
    Result: Nothing lives only in your head 24 hours after a call.
  5. 5. Morning brief

    Your daily orientation. Pulls Google Calendar and Gmail, surfaces yesterday's open loops, shows the day's anchor + top 3 focus items + stale-lead watch. One page.

    Trigger: "morning brief" or "what's today"
    Claude pulls everything and writes to _planning/daily/{date}-morning.md.
    Result: Two minutes to know what matters today. The simplified version of the daily brief Daunte runs at BPP.

Your First Two WeeksA pace that gets you running without burning out.

Day 1
Install Google Drive for Desktop. Install Claude Desktop. Unzip the workspace template into G:\My Drive\. Open CLAUDE.md and edit anything that doesn't sound like you. First draft is fine.
Days 2–3
Turn on Cowork inside Claude Desktop and point it at your workspace folder. Ask Claude to read your business and tell you what it doesn't understand. Use the gaps to improve CLAUDE.md.
Week 1
Run business-card-scanner on every card from your next BNI meeting. Confirm the workflow feels natural. Move two existing active clients into 2. Clients/ using the template.
Week 2
Run walkthrough-to-proposal on your next real walkthrough. Compare the output to what you'd get from ChatGPT. Run morning-brief three mornings in a row to feel the difference.
Week 3+
By now you've found the rough edges. Loop Daunte in for a 30-minute working session to sharpen the skills, adjust CLAUDE.md, or add a new skill that fits a pattern you've spotted.
The honest pace

Week one is investment. By week two you're moving faster than you were before. The gap widens every week. Don't try to install and master everything in one sitting. That's how people quit.
