      8Quarterly Reviewthe story behind the numbers
      How this works & what to trust
      One data model, sliced live. Every tile and chart recomputes from monthly records for the year / quarter / month you pick. Revenue, expenses, net income are QuickBooks cash basis (the accurate money view) and reconcile to the monthly close. Deals won/lost follow HubSpot close dates — that's bookings momentum, not cash, so it won't match same-month revenue. One deliberate override: Financial Acuity's $3,350 is attributed to Q1 (its first invoice, January) to match the team's ratified quarterly attribution, not HubSpot's later May close date. Social & website come from Metricool; social history starts Sept 2025 and website Nov 2025, so earlier months read low or blank. Source archives live in DA-003 (social) and DA-004 (financial).
